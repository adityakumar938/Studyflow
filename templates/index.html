The StudyFlow loop

From two PDFs to a weighted plan.

1

Add your PDFs

Place syllabus.pdf and pyq.pdf in the project root — the same files the API already reads.

2

AI extracts structure

Gemini pulls topics with their units from the syllabus, and every question from the paper.

3

Questions meet topics

Each PYQ is mapped to its syllabus topic, producing real per-unit exam weightage.

4

Hours follow weightage

Enter your total hours — StudyFlow splits them proportionally and exports a PDF plan.
